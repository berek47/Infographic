There exists an optimal generator load: too light and the swing's energy is wasted, too heavy and the pendulum stalls before it can give it up.

§1The governing equation

The motion is that of a damped physical pendulum. Two torques drain it, each proportional to angular velocity: useful extraction at the generator and parasitic loss at the pivot.

m·L²·θ̈ = −m·g·L·sinθ − (cp + cg)·θ̇(1)

Power divides cleanly between the two channels, accumulated each step by the integrator:

Pgen = cg·θ̇2 ,   Ploss = cp·θ̇2(2)

The ledger above is nothing more than conservation of energy made visible: whatever departs the swing arrives as harvest, as heat in the pivot, or remains as motion.

§2What the model rewards

At release the pendulum holds E₀ = m·g·L·(1 − cos θ₀). Efficiency η is the fraction of that the generator captures before all motion ceases.

θ
angular position measured from vertical (rad)
θ̇
angular velocity, the sole driver of output power
cg
generator load, the extraction you keep
cp
pivot damping, the loss you engineer against
L
rod length, fixes both period and stored energy

The lever this study examines: raise cg to take more from each swing, but past the optimum the pendulum scarcely moves and total harvest falls away. The task is to find that peak.
